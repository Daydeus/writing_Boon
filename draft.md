## The Ritual
Skylar stood in a row amongst five other teenagers under the hot summer sun.

This revelation.

You couldn't reason with an infant.

Everyone waited for the priest to come from the inn.

Every so often, Skylar would straighten his back and stared ahead at the inn, forcing himself to un-wilt from the heat.
Skylar wasn't a terribly big fan of the sun.
When the sky grew dark and roiled with lightning was when he felt most alive.

The presence of the Great World Spirit saturated him, leaving him gasping as he felt something sear itself into his mind.
The words were white-hot and he didn't dare think about them for a long while.

[Weather Ward]

[Weather Ward] - You may forbid the presence of weather effects in the space around you.

'This was NOT a combat ability,' he evaluated.
'This is an ability a farmer would be grateful for, even ecstatic.'
Skylar though his father was a farmer now, had ambitions.
His father had hopes ofr Skylar's future.
And this Boon, while fantastic, did nothing to aide him in that journey.
Skylar's throat tightened at the thought of sharing his boon with his father.
The path forward, as narrow and hazardous as it was already, just grew significantly more difficult.
